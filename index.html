About D.Genz

Made for the New Generation.

Our story

Genz is a restaurant built for the new generation. We started with a simple idea: great food should be fresh, bold, and affordable, in a place where you actually want to hang out.

Every dish is made to order with quality ingredients and a lot of flavor. Whether you’re stopping by for a quick lunch, meeting friends after a long day, or ordering in, we make sure every plate is worth it.

We cook with quality ingredients, prepared fresh every day.

Hungry people don’t like waiting, so your food reaches you hot and on time.

Good food, good music, good company. Genz is made for sharing.
